What Prixum does

Everything Prism had is still there. This is what's new.

Skins that walk

A large player you can turn with the mouse and all your saved skins next to it, each one walking in place. Add a skin from a file, by player name or with a NameMC link.

The skin history of a Microsoft account loads by itself, so you never hunt for old skins again.

Wallpaper and frosted glass

Put your own picture behind the instance list and the tiles turn into frosted glass. Blur and dimming are adjustable, with a preview right in the settings.

Off by default: "Settings → Appearance → Wallpaper".

A theme for every mood

Seven built-in Nova themes, from the pink Prixum to AMOLED, plus the classic Prism themes. The theme editor changes colors, corners and the font, and the launcher redraws right away.

The icons follow the theme colors.

Settings where everything is easy to find

Pages are grouped, and the search looks through every page at once and highlights what it finds. Check boxes became switches, themes are picked from cards.

Ctrl+F opens the search.

And more

  • Render scalingThe game renders at a lower resolution and stretches to the window: more FPS or a pixel look. Through gamescope on Linux.
  • Instances with mods from the startThe version, the loader, mods, resource packs and shaders in one window.
  • Modrinth and CurseForgeCards with authors and download counts, update checks, turning mods on and off in bulk.
  • Microsoft and Ely.bySeveral accounts, and Ely.by for servers that use it.
  • Moving over from PrismOn the first start Prixum copies your instances, accounts and settings. It leaves the Prism folder alone.
  • Open sourcePrixum is free, the code is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0.
